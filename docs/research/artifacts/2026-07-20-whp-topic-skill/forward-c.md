## Why the runner-up lost

The formal runner-up is **social-media likes — how public like counts become status points and change what people post**.

| Strongest package and payoff | **Your Job Has a Secret Scoreboard** reveals a second hierarchy and gives a four-part map | **Likes Are Status Points** reveals public feedback as rank and explains posting behavior | Both package cleanly; the workplace payoff applies to a broader recurring adult situation and is less likely to attract an audience expecting a dopamine/addiction video. |

Workplace evidence includes a review, experimental program, hierarchy research, and organizational meta-analysis; social evidence is more platform- and age-specific, with a key three-platform source still a working paper. |
| Best next episode | Directly satisfies the mandate for a maximally human, useful Hidden Game pilot and demonstrates the brand beyond technology criticism | Also fits, but risks placing WHP immediately in the saturated anti-social-media/dopamine neighborhood | Workplace status introduces the lens more broadly and leaves social scoring as a natural follow-up. |

**Reversal condition:** Promote social-media likes if the paired package/comprehension test clearly favors it and the workplace package cannot attract viewers without drifting into tactical or universal claims.

**What happened to the supplied Sudoku finalist:** Sudoku was not demoted for weak subject quality. It passed game centrality, human revelation, payoff, evidence, and production gates. It failed only portfolio fit: `BRAND.md` requires the pre-launch pilot to be a Hidden Game. Public evidence is genuinely mixed—exact global-history videos observed at roughly 3.2K–37.6K, while adjacent Sudoku curiosity videos reached roughly 0.52M–3.68M (R11); a live World Championship in October supplies a timing hook (R12). Once WHP has launched with a Hidden Game, Sudoku should be reconsidered as the leading explicit-game episode, with a package built around its language-free global spread rather than generic history.
