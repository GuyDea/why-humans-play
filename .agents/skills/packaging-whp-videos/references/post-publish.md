# Post-Publish Packaging

Live data is the only CTR truth. Read it correctly, test deliberately, and feed every
outcome back into the packaging record.

## Read CTR correctly

- Read **per traffic source** (Browse, Suggested, Search) against the channel's own
  baseline, jointly with average view duration — never as one raw number.
- Windows: first 24–48h per surface, then the 7-day settle. Half of all videos land
  between 2% and 10% CTR; small-channel CTR runs deceptively high on warm
  impressions, and **falling CTR with rising impressions is usually growth** (colder
  audiences), not failure.
- Rough health bands while the channel is small: Browse ~3.5–4.5% is healthy, 7%+
  exceptional; Suggested ~5–10%. High CTR with collapsed retention means the package
  over-promised — that is a packaging defect even though the clicks came.

## Test & Compare

- Up to 3 variants (thumbnails; titles and title+thumbnail combos since Dec 2025),
  shown concurrently; the winner is decided by **watch-time share**, not raw CTR;
  runs days up to ~2 weeks. Editing packaging mid-test kills the test.
- Test **diverse concepts** — the trio's distinct routes exist for this; near-twins
  prolong tests and teach nothing.
- Requires Advanced Features; until unlocked, run manual swaps: change the package,
  compare the next 24–48h window per surface against the previous one.
- Practice on older videos first when the feature is new to the channel.

## Repackage triggers

Repackaging a live video is the best-documented ROI lever on YouTube (Veritasium's
Magnus-effect flop → 16M+ on a new package, video unchanged).

- Sustained under ~2% CTR on its main surface with healthy retention → the package
  fails the episode: build a new trio (a scoped **Generate packages** run seeded with
  what the live data rejected) and swap or test.
- Healthy CTR, collapsed early retention → the package over-promises: fix the
  promise, not the polish.
- Evergreen video decaying after months → refresh the package; treat as a new trio
  against the original.
- A half may move alone here, and only here: hold the thumbnail, test titles (or the
  reverse) as a controlled experiment. Record which half moved and why.

## Close the loop

Append to the episode's packaging record after every window read, test, and swap:
date, surface numbers, what changed, what won, and the one-line lesson. When a lesson
generalizes beyond the episode, route it into doctrine through the repository's
reconciliation flow — live results are the only calibration data this channel owns,
and they compound only if recorded.
