# Outlier Packaging Research

Research what packaging already wins for this topic before designing any package.
Patterns, not single winners; structures remixed, never copied.

## Method

1. **Map the landscape.** Web-search the exact topic and its adjacent framings (the
   subject, the mechanism, the audience's own words for the problem). Collect the
   videos a viewer would actually see next to ours: same-topic, adjacent-topic, and
   the strongest performers from channels our target viewer watches. Aim for 15–40
   videos across 8–20 channels, niche plus adjacent.
2. **Estimate outlier status.** For each candidate, compare its views to the
   channel's typical recent videos (median of visible recent uploads is enough).
   Record the multiple. Treat <2x as noise, ≥3x as signal; a small channel's 20x
   beats a big channel's 2x, because the audience cannot explain it. Views are a
   distribution-confounded proxy — that is exactly why only clustered patterns count.
3. **Cluster the ≥3x outliers by pattern**, never analyze one video alone:
   - **Title syntax:** structure, not words — question vs statement, first vs second
     vs third person, number use, negation, length, the curiosity device.
   - **Thumbnail framing:** route (face-led / pure-concept / brand-anchor /
     pattern-reveal), element count, overlay word count, palette family, emotion
     register.
   - **Package logic:** what the thumbnail shows vs what the title tells; the one
     question the pair opens.
   A pattern needs ≥2 independent channels to count. Note anti-patterns too: what the
   losers in the same results share.
4. **Extract 3–5 remixable structures.** For each: the abstract structure, two
   outlier examples with their multiples, and one WHP-native remix direction that
   stays inside BRAND.md's register. Copying a surface (same words, same image idea)
   is prohibited; remixing a structure is the point.

## The packaging-patterns brief (output contract)

Return exactly this shape, ready to paste into the packaging record:

```markdown
### Packaging-patterns brief — <topic> — <date>
- **Landscape:** N videos / M channels searched; the 3 titles our package will sit
  next to most often.
- **Outliers:** table of ≥3x videos — channel, title, views vs baseline, multiple.
- **Winning patterns:** 3–5 clustered structures, each with examples, multiples, and
  a WHP remix direction.
- **Anti-patterns:** what underperformers share.
- **Competitor thumbnails:** saved images (packaging/mockups/competitors/) for feed
  mockups, with source URLs.
- **Confidence:** how much signal the sample actually held; name thin spots honestly.
```

Sampling honesty: view counts and upload baselines come from search results and
channel pages, not privileged data — label estimates as estimates, and never present
a single outlier as a proven formula.
