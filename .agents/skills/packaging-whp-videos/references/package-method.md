# Package Generation and Selection

## Ideate 15–20 packages

Each candidate is one unit conceived as a single two-part promise: the thumbnail half
shows the tension, the title half tells it, and together they open exactly one
question the episode honestly answers. Write them together — drafting a title pool
first and matching images later (or the reverse) is a contract violation.

Cover the space deliberately rather than free-associating:

- **Route coverage:** candidates across at least three thumbnail routes — face-led,
  pure-concept, brand-anchor, pattern-reveal.
- **Emotion coverage:** curiosity-led, stakes/loss-led, and paradox-led title halves;
  avoid fear-alarm framing (it measurably hurts clicks and is off-register).
- **Pattern grounding:** when a packaging-patterns brief exists, at least half the
  candidates remix its winning structures; the rest may explore. Note each candidate's
  source pattern or mark it `exploratory`.
- **Stance coverage:** include first-person narrator candidates ("I tested…", "what I
  found…") where the stake is honest — evidence favors them and the channel is
  founder-presented.

For each candidate record: ID; title half; thumbnail half (route, visual, overlay ≤3
words, one-glance read); the one question the pair opens; the episode beat that
answers it.

## Score each package as a unit

Score and record with dead tests named; a dead test kills the candidate.

1. **Title half — feed-adapted gate** (from the blueprint packaging owner,
   `writing-whp-youtube-scripts/references/script-blueprint-workflow.md`): T1 stake
   the target viewer recognizes as their own (second-person phrasing not required,
   earns no credit by itself; first-person may carry the stake); T2 unclosable gap;
   T3 edge placement; T4 unhedged stakes. 0–2 each; any T1–T3 zero kills.
2. **Title half — feed constraints:** ≤ ~60 chars, decisive words in first ~40;
   specific-but-incomplete calibration; no question-bait smell; no promise the
   episode cannot pay.
3. **Thumbnail half — one-glance lint:** ≤3 elements, one focal point; overlay ≤3
   words and not repeating the title; readable at 160×90; survives grayscale; works
   on light and dark feed; face (if any) large enough to read emotion, gaze pointed
   at the key object; brand palette compatible.
4. **Pair — complementarity:** each half carries weight the other cannot. If the
   title still works with a blank gray image, the thumbnail half is decorative — kill
   or redesign. If the thumbnail is fully explained by the title, same.
5. **Honesty:** name the exact beat that pays the opened question. No beat, no
   candidate.

## Select three winners

Pick the three highest-scoring packages that span **distinct thumbnail routes** — the
trio's job at publish is a real experiment, and near-identical variants prolong tests
and teach nothing. If two top scorers share a route, keep the better and promote the
best candidate from another route. Record why each winner won and what its selection
sacrifices. The three winners are the Test & Compare trio; ranking is a
recommendation — the choice is Martin's.
