# Why You Can Game for 6 Hours but Can't Study for 10 Minutes

## 1. The two nights

[MAIN HOOK | LOOP OPEN L-01 | LOCKED WORDING — Opens the title question as the viewer's own lived asymmetry; the final beat must close it as a verdict on the task, not the viewer.]

> <u>**Why can you focus on a game for six hours — but not on ten minutes of the studying you actually want to do?**</u>

[OBSTACLE — The contrast-pair felt entry: both memories are the viewer's own recent weeks; no simulation, no single-scene reconstruction (canon variety vs ep001/old-ep002 routes).]

> <u>You know both nights.</u> The night a game swallowed six hours, and they felt like twenty minutes. And the night ten minutes of studying felt like an hour — until you found something, anything, else to do.

[DEFENSE — The family-argument script from the audience scan, voiced as recognition, then the three folk verdicts the demolition beat will test.]

> Maybe someone has even said it to you: "If you can game all night, you could study if you really tried." Maybe you say it to yourself.
>
> There are three ready verdicts for those nights — you're lazy, you're addicted, or your attention is broken.

[MINI-HOOK — Holds the ADHD segment early so the largest existing frame doesn't bounce; pays at the exchange-rate boundary in SP02 and the hyperfocus line in SP05.]

> *If your word is ADHD, hold that thought. It's part of this story, and it gets its honest place.*

[LOOP OPEN L-02 — The withheld number: the intention null that ends the laziness story; pays at the SP02 demolition.]

> <u>Then researchers pooled almost seven hundred results on why people put things off. All three verdicts fell apart. One number in that pile ends the laziness story on its own. I'll show it to you.</u>

[PROMISE | LOCKED WORDING — The literal two-half learning contract: the understanding (what the minute-ten struggle is) and the concrete response (the four-question check plus one lever), bounded to what the intervention evidence supports ("try tonight," not "fix").]

> **By the end of this video, you'll know what actually stops you at minute ten — and it isn't weak will.**
>
> **And you'll leave with a four-question check that finds what's missing, plus one evidence-backed move you can try tonight.**

[LOOP OPEN L-03 — The withheld ingredient: chosen challenge, isolated by the assigned-vs-chosen difficulty experiments; pays at the SP04 reveal after the payment list is demonstrated.]

> <u>Because games add one hidden ingredient to hard things — an ingredient studying almost never gets. Twenty thousand game sessions exposed it. And it's the one you can steal back.</u>

- **Evidence/story job and boundary:** E-01, E-02. Demolition, not inversion: never "task beats traits" — conscientiousness (−.62) named aloud, seeding the exchange-rate line.
