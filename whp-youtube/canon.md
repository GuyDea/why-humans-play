# WHP Channel Canon

The channel-level memory that lets episodes compound into a brand instead of standing
alone. Three registries: named concepts, opening shapes, and the assumed-knowledge
baseline. Consult before drafting; update at each episode's promotion into `draft/`.

- **Concept rule:** before coining a name in narration, check the canon. Reuse the
  existing name instead of a synonym; a canon concept's locked wording is delivered
  word-perfect when the concept returns. Register new episode-coined concepts here at
  promotion.
- **Variety rule:** at the Blueprint stage, check the opening-shape ledger. Do not
  repeat the previous episode's route + withheld-object combination; vary at least one
  axis so returning viewers cannot predict the intro's shape.
- **Baseline rule:** do not spend narration explaining anything in the
  assumed-knowledge baseline. When an episode must exceed the baseline, teach it once,
  plainly, and move on.

## Assumed-knowledge baseline

The WHP viewer is a curious adult who uses technology daily. Do not explain:

- what ChatGPT or an AI chatbot is, or that chatbots sometimes "hallucinate" / make
  things up (name the behavior, skip the tutorial);
- what a radiologist, psychologist, or researcher is;
- what a study, experiment, control group, or placebo is in general terms (explain a
  *specific* design only where the design is the story);
- everyday technology, games, and internet culture.

Teach on first use (once, plainly): named psychological effects (framing, sycophancy,
processing fluency, anchoring, self-handicapping), medical specifics (what a TIA is),
and any WHP-coined concept at its first appearance.

Update this baseline when audience data or comments show it is wrong in either
direction.
